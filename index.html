How to Play

The goal: hold 60% of the world's regions, or lead all empires in trade income for 5 straight seasons. Lose your capital and every port, and your line ends.

Each season: your income arrives automatically. Then act freely — tap any region or fleet to command it. End the season when you're done; rivals move, and the Gazette reports what happened.

Armies: muster regiments in your regions (men come from your population), then march them into neighbouring regions to conquer. Forts, walls and terrain matter. Your army's tech tier multiplies its strength.

Fleets: build warships in ports with shipyards. Fleets sail between sea zones; park one off an enemy port to blockade it — a blockaded port earns no trade and slowly bleeds men.

Trade: lanes between your ports pay every season, thicker lanes pay more. Conquer a port and its lanes become yours. Allies open their ports to your lanes.

The Crown: momentous matters — war warnings, offers, crises — wait under the 👑 Crown button. Only big decisions reach you.
