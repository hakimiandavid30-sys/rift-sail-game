Orders of the Crown

Speak plainly — name an army, a squadron, a city, a governor. Try: “move my Lisbon army and attack León” · “send my squadron to blockade Algiers” · “put Reyes on Porto for wealth” · “found a colony at Chesdin” · “show the winds”. Say cancel to unwrite the last order — the Contrarian’s Quill holds 2 charges a season.

How to Play

The goal: hold 60% of the world's regions, or lead all empires in trade income for 5 straight seasons. Lose your capital and every port, and your line ends.

Time flows: the speed bar at the foot of the chart sets the world moving. Days pass; seasons (90 days) still bring income and the Gazette. Armies and fleets travel in real time — pause whenever you want to think. Big moments pause the world for you (toggle: Auto-pause).

Armies: muster regiments into a region's garrison, then Form a Field Army and March it — tap any lit region and the army walks the roads there, day by day. Garrisons always stay behind. Park an army with Hold this Ground to block a road. Forts, walls and terrain matter in battle.

Fleets: build warships in ports with shipyards. Sail a squadron to a port or open sea; a blockade only counts when your ships actually sit off the enemy harbour — then its trade dies and the town bleeds.

Trade: lanes between your ports pay every season, thicker lanes pay more. Conquer a port and its lanes become yours. Allies open their ports to your lanes.

The Crown: momentous matters — war warnings, offers, crises — wait under the Crown button. Only big decisions reach you.
